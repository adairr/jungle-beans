Jungle Beans

Live the Bean!

The Discovery

Deep in the Amazon jungle, a research expedition stumbled onto something the pharmaceutical world swore couldn't exist: Phaseolus radiculi — the Jungle Bean. Locals had known about it for generations. Science finally caught up.

The bean's effects read like a hoax: viral remediation, cancer remediation, and a deep, reliable sense of blissfulness, all from a legume you could grow in a backyard plot. No patent. No exclusivity. No billion-dollar clinical trial pipeline required.

The Ban

That was the problem. A cure that can't be owned is a cure that can't be sold at a markup — and Mega Pharma doesn't do "can't." Within a year, country after country quietly outlawed the import, sale, and cultivation of Jungle Beans, citing "public safety." The beans didn't stop working. They just stopped being legal.

Your Role

You're building a distribution network anyway — one airport, one sale, one close call at a time. Ten cities, one wallet, one life meter, and a world of customs agents, mobsters, and bad weather standing between you and a global supply chain for the one plant that actually helps people.

No legal cover. No corporate backing. Just you, a suitcase, and a blissfully stubborn bean that refuses to stay banned.
